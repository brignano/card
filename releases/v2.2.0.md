## npx brignano 2.2.0

The card catches up with the latest [`@brignano/design`](https://github.com/brignano/design), with a calmer splash and a menu that follows your cursor. Run `npx brignano` to see it.

### A menu that follows your cursor
- The highlight isn't a single lit row any more. The row under the cursor is brightest and its neighbours step down through the same blue, so the emphasis moves with you as you arrow up and down.
- Everything else works as before: arrows wrap at both ends, and you can jump by typing a number or the start of an option's name.

### A calmer splash
- The amber monogram no longer climbs into pale yellow at the right edge. Same light sweep, easier on the eyes.

### Colours from design 0.6.0
- Links and the menu use the design system's own cobalt instead of a stock blue.
- On light-background terminals, the card's labels (GitHub, LinkedIn, Website, Email) are now dark enough to read comfortably. They used to fall below the contrast the design system requires for text.

### A lighter install
- `npx brignano` downloads 95 packages instead of 110.
