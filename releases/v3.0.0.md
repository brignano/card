## npx brignano 3.0.0

The card now installs in a blink, reads well on light terminals, and its links and QR codes work wherever you run it. Run `npx brignano` to see it.

### Needs Node 22.18 or newer
- Node 20 reached end of life in April 2026, so the card no longer supports it. That's the only reason this is a major version: nothing else about how you run it changes.

### Much faster to start
- `npx brignano` now downloads one package instead of about a hundred: 1.4 MB instead of 28 MB. The card appears in under a tenth of a second.

### Made for light terminals too
- The card asks your terminal what colour its background is and picks the light or dark palette to match. Before, most light terminals got near-white text on a white background.
- The QR codes (`--qr` and the contact code in the menu) now scan on light terminals. They used to be drawn as a negative there, which phone cameras can't read.

### Links you can click
- GitHub, LinkedIn, Website, Email and the latest project are real links in terminals that support them, so the email address opens your mail app.

### Smoother and fresher
- The banner animation no longer flickers on slower terminals or over SSH.
- The tagline and the fun fact are now picked separately, so you see more combinations.
- Colours are up to date with `@brignano/design` 0.9.0.
